Direction 1: VANGUARD CREST. A bold upward-sweeping asymmetric title silhouette on a small deep teal shield backing; two chunky ivory-and-gold swords cross behind the wordmark with teal leather handles visible at lower outer corners. A tall faceted cyan diamond seated in an angular gold crown at the top center. Small wing-like cream armor plates flare outward, suggestive of an advancing fantasy army. The title is larger than its backing; the shield is only visible above and below, not a full rectangle. Energetic battle-ready but friendly. Rich layered depth, compact and readable.
